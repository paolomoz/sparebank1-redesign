# Improvements — sparebank1.no (variant A brief, brand-faithful)

Descriptive of the gap between the captured site and a competent 2026 execution of the same brand. Each item: measurable observation · pattern at fault · one concrete fix. Prototype shape briefs cite items by number.

1. **[dated pattern] The alliance router is a 400 px illustrated landscape that precedes every page's own headline.** `bank-choice` landmark rect `top 129, height 400` on 70/100 pages; the product H1 ("Boliglån") lands at `top 527–539` at 1440×900, i.e. below 58 % of the first viewport, and the market-landing H1 is `visually-hidden`. Pattern: full-height promotional band as a global chrome element. Fix: keep the router in the first viewport (IA priority § 8 — audience routing) but as a **compact band ≤ 96 px** (postcode field, "Bruk min posisjon", "Se alle banker" inline); the captured landscape illustration (`bankchoice_bg.svg`) is kept at reduced scale on ≥ 1024 px as the signature motif (§ 8b) and hidden < 768 px exactly as captured.
2. **[cluttered IA] Two-row 129 px header with 3 all-caps top links + 9 market links + 3 CTAs.** `header__wrap` rect height 129; top nav `PRIVAT / BEDRIFT / OM OSS` uppercase (the only uppercase on the site, `headingsUppercasePercent` ≈ 1 %); `Søk`, `Bli kunde`, `Logg inn` all styled as pills of different colours (grey outline / green fill / green outline). Pattern: everything-at-once masthead. Fix: one 72 px row — logo, market nav (9 links verbatim), a quiet sentence-case audience switch (Privat · Bedrift · Om oss), search as an icon-button with visible label, **one** filled action (Logg inn, Skog green) and Bli kunde as a text-pill secondary. Collapses to the stock hamburger at ≤ 640 px.
3. **[type scale] Ad-hoc heading ramp with no ratio.** `T-scale`: 46 → 36 → 28 → 22 → 18 → 16 (ratios 1.278 / 1.286 / 1.273 / 1.222 / 1.125); all faces weight 400, so H1 (46) vs H2 (36) is the only hierarchy cue and reads flat at 1440. Pattern: size-only hierarchy on a compressed ramp. Fix: **1.25 modular scale** on the pinned faces — 61 / 49 / 39 / 31 / 25 / 20 / 16 / 13 — display line-height 1.1, lead 20/30, prose measure 68ch; the title face carries display + headline, the medium face carries titles, regular carries body.
4. **[cliché convention] Four card systems on one product page, all white-with-shadow.** boliglan: `prices` cards (10 loan variants), `related-topics` newsfeed cards (4, kicker "TIPS OG RÅD" as eyebrow), price-and-terms, visual-nav; 66/100 pages carry ≥ 1 card grid; card = white, 5 px radius, `0 1px 4px rgba(38,38,38,.3)` shadow, image top, title, one line, chevron. Pattern: card-as-container for everything. Fix: **one card language** — flat at rest, ground = tinted paper (Sand-30 / Frost-30) or a hairline, radius 16 px, exactly one action per card (whole card is the link), kicker moves to a meta line under the title (never an eyebrow above it), photo at native 3:2 with the brand's large-radius mask on a single corner pair.
5. **[contrast / a11y] Headline over photograph with a single gradient scrim; grey on tints at the AA line.** news-article hero (`nb-bank-om-oss-nyheter-*`): the only gradient on the site (`linear-gradient(rgba(0,0,0,0)…`, 1 occurrence) carries a white H1 over a photo — legibility depends on the photo's luminance (toolkit § 1 "hero text on photographic background without contrast scrim"). Secondary text `#676767` on Frost-30 `#d8e9f2` computes ≈ 4.5:1, at the body floor. Fix: article heroes put the headline **beside or below** the photo (photo as content); secondary text on tinted grounds uses Koksgrå `#323232` (≥ 9:1).
6. **[missed opportunity] Real-people photography rendered as 302×200 thumbnails from 1280-px sources.** boliglan carries 6 photos; 5 are rendered at `w 302, h 200` (`slots.images`) from `…thumb.1280.1280.jpg`; Flow A measured `object-fit: cover; object-position: 50% 0` stretching 768×575 → 310×200. Pattern: decorative thumbnail. Fix: the hero photo renders at ≥ 715 px wide, 3:2, uncropped; card photos keep native ratio (`aspect-ratio: 3/2`, `object-fit: cover` centred); one photograph per module, never two competing.
7. **[density] 11 stacked bands separated by `<hr>` on the market landing.** nb-bank-privat: 11 `sections` (campaign carousel, 3 column-grids, hr ×3, LOfavør banner, related-topics rail, cobranding…), 75 px band padding ×11, `documentHeight 4283`. Pattern: band-per-component with rule dividers. Fix: group into **5 movements** (offer · products · help · membership · news), 64 px section rhythm, dividers removed, tint changes (white / Sand-30 / Frost-30) mark movements — never two tinted bands of the same hue adjacent.
